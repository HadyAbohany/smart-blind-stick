# Demonstration sequence

1. Photograph and weigh the V1 cane and the V2 additions. Record the firmware revision and supply.
2. Test local warning against a nonmoving reference obstacle, then repeat with phone off. Use a handheld meter and a second observer to time cue onset.
3. Place the probe over a shallow test tray. Trigger wet, dry it, then overlap wet and near. Check near wins.
4. Pair the bench phone and show range, wet, and loss/reconnect. Join the private camera AP, show an agreed class and unplug the camera to exercise degradation.
5. Simulate a fall, cancel before timeout, then exercise mock expiry. Use the demo SMS draft path for the public presentation. Show logs or a bench-run recording for real delivery.
6. Share cost, p95 delay, detection failures, and measured runtime. Discard personal numbers and footage after the presentation according to the protocol.
