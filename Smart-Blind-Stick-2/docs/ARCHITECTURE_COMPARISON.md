# Architecture comparison

الحسبة بتشمل local controller منفصل في الاختيارات كلها، عشان تعطل الـ vision ميوقفش الاهتزاز. تكلفة المنظومة من غير الـ vision branch تساوي 2,010 جنيه من الـ BOM، وبتشمل bank 10,000 mAh والـ mounts. كل الأرقام من غير تمن جسم العصاية أو الموبايل. Incremental يفترض إعادة استخدام HC-SR04 يوفر 40 جنيه. أطوال التشغيل تستخدم 23.8 Wh مع reserve. الوزن تقدير للإضافات من غير العصاية أو الموبايل.

**Reliability and connectivity.** A keeps radio duties on separate boards. Use a private WPA2 AP for JPEG and BLE GATT for telemetry. Android requests local Wi-Fi with a `WifiNetworkSpecifier` and binds each camera request to that network; SMS remains on a cellular SIM. Coexistence is still device-dependent. Same-band interference, Android prompts, disconnections and no-internet Wi-Fi need tests. BLE works for 18-byte reads at 4 Hz. At 5 FPS a 30 kB JPEG stream pushes 1.2 Mbit/s before overhead. Fragmentation, connection intervals and retransmission make BLE video unsuitable for this prototype.

B runs the detector offline before phone pairing and can publish categories via BLE or through a local ESP32 bridge. Android still handles contacts, GPS and dispatch. Heat and brownout recovery are bigger risks than A. C can classify offline and relay detections over UART to the ESP32, with Android integration equivalent at the BLE boundary. Validate vendor model ops, latency under sustained capture and the camera FPC early. D requires a particular phone's USB host and video support, cable strain management, and a charging strategy while USB is occupied. A local Wi-Fi camera variant of D effectively becomes A. All four preserve local warning over phone or network failure.

Choose **A** for this build. Local sourcing and an isolated firmware path are stronger near-term advantages than an integrated NPU or completely phone-independent classification. Use D only if the target handset fails the Wi-Fi/BLE test and a borrowed UVC camera passes host/latency and cable tests. If the requirement later becomes vision without a smartphone, benchmark a borrowed Luckfox or Pi 5 before purchasing.
