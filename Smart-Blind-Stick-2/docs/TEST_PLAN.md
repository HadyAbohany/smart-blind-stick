# Acceptance and test plan

These limits are proposed prototype gates. Record measured distributions and failures in [bench log](../prototypes/bench-results.tsv) and raw vision observations in [vision log](../ai/evaluation/observations.csv). Use serial logs, a DMM, scope, USB energy meter, measured fixtures, a light meter and two Android phones. Keep V1 as a repeatable comparator.

| Test | Procedure | Gate / metrics |
|---|---|---|
| Electrical bring-up | Check every net cold, amp-limit 5 V, check trigger and divider into high impedance, add each branch, measure 5 V at both boards with motor start and active Wi-Fi | Rail 4.75–5.25 V at boards, echo within DevKit logic and absolute limits, no resets or excessive heating. Record peak and inrush. |
| Range accuracy | At 20/40/60/90/105/150/200 cm marked distance, run 30 pings each at normal room temperature on rigid board | Median absolute error ≤5 cm on rigid board at 20–150 cm. Report p95 and missed returns separately. |
| Near warning latency | Introduce calibrated flat target from outside cone at 60 cm, record target marker and motor current/transducer with time-coded video or scope, repeat ≥30 times | p95 first physical haptic onset ≤180 ms, with phone connected/disconnected and during camera stream. |
| Blind spots | Grid heights and angles across beam, slim pole, cloth, glass at angles, shiny surfaces, leg-like objects, low objects and cane sweep. Record velocity, hit/miss and sustained first detection | ≥90% hit rate for rigid 30 cm-wide obstacle within mapped ±7° at 0.3–1.2 m. Publish the failure map including low/soft/angled objects. |
| Water | Dry, wet tile, shallow tray, distilled/tap/salty water, mud and droplets. Twenty dry/wet cycles each. Repeat after 100 wet/dry cycles. | ≥18/20 tap-water direct contacts detected within 600 ms. Dry release ≤1 s after electrodes wiped. Record conductivity misses and damp residue false alerts. |
| Haptic cue recognition | Randomize near and wet cues over at least 10 trials per cue in a seated test; adjust fit and amplitude afterward | ≥90% two-cue recognition target. Log confusion, masking, glove/hand positions and interruptions. |
| Vision accuracy and lighting | Annotated scenes across 20–50 lx, 100–300 lx and daylight, including clutter, movement and negatives. At least 30 relevant positives per primary class | Daylight priority label precision ≥0.8 and recall ≥0.7 before expanding. Report low-light metrics independently. If quality falls short, disable speech in affected conditions after a light/blur gate is validated. |
| Vision timing and false speech | See AI evaluation plan, 15 min sustained stream with BLE, log temperature and external event marker. Include walking motion and hard negatives. | ≥5 processed FPS, p95 ≤500 ms capture→decision, p95 ≤1 s to first context after gating. ≤1 false spoken cue per 5 min controlled route at launch target. |
| BLE communication | Log matched changes in scope/serial and UI. Turn stick off/on and disable radios independently, including while streaming. Repeat 20 times per phone. | p95 telemetry update ≤600 ms, stale display clears ≤4 s after read timeout, reconnect ≤10 s in ≥18/20 valid range trials after powered radio is advertising. |
| Offline and degradation | Turn off cellular data and remove internet. Verify local WPA2 camera with BLE, then switch phone and camera off and run 30 low-risk obstacles. | 30/30 near cues for qualifying reference target and no increase beyond local onset threshold. Vision requires the phone. Cellular voice/SMS evaluation independent. |
| Power | Run USB energy meter at minimal, representative and high alert rates, capture phone drain independently. Measure weight with mounted bank. | ≥6 h stick operation with camera in typical test, no low-load shutoff in 2 h local idle, ≤400 g added mass initial target, comfortable sweep after supervised review. |
| Falls/false positives | Play scripted IMU fixtures and actuate padded phone jig for expected fast-fall patterns. Daily motions: sit, lie, stairs as ordinary activity, swing, transit, phone drop, phone left on table. No staged human falls. | ≥90% fast-fall trace detection on held-out traces and ≤1 false candidate per 2 h of representative daily activities as a preliminary gate. Phone drop and slow falls are explicitly reported. |
| SOS and cancellation | Exercise phone, button and mock. Test cancel early and near expiry, real expiry, no contacts, revoked permission, indoor GNSS, dual SIM unset, weak/no coverage, link outage and stale button event. | 0 messages for mock or cancelled event across 20 reps. 10/10 lab messages received within 60 s after expiry under stable service, with recipient timestamps. No false delivery status offline. |
| Accessibility | Complete pairing, contact entry, checking statuses, countdown cancellation and draft under TalkBack, enlarged font, one hand and muted TTS | Each task completes without visual-only clues; record time, blocked controls and focus order. Local alert cues remain distinguishable. |

Obtain institutional ethics review as applicable before human data collection, and informed accessible consent and safe mobility supervision. Start seated or at a controlled obstacle course with a spotter and the normal cane. Exclude stairs, roads, intentional falls and hazardous water trials from human scenarios. Anonymize observations and retain only agreed recordings. Stop trials on unsafe behavior, discomfort or confusing warnings.
